Marketing

Scrolling Content

Infinite CSS marquee for rotating taglines — no JS, pauses on hover, respects reduced motion.

Problem: merchants want an eye-catching strip of rotating taglines or trust signals, but a JS-driven carousel is heavier than the content deserves and can jank on slower devices.

Solution: a pure-CSS marquee — the phrase list is duplicated once and animated with a single translateX keyframe for a seamless loop, with speed/direction settings, hover-to-pause, and an automatic pause under prefers-reduced-motion.

Media Gallery

Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar

Keeps the buy button in view on long product pages, synced live with the selected variant.

Free Shipping Progress Bar

Live cart-page progress bar tracking how close shoppers are to free shipping.

Problem: merchants want to nudge shoppers toward a free-shipping minimum, but a static "Free shipping over $50" banner doesn't react to what's actually in the cart.

Personalization

Recently Viewed Products

Remembers browsed products via localStorage and re-fetches fresh price/image data.

Problem: shoppers who browse several products often want to circle back to one, but Shopify doesn't track that for them out of the box.

Solution: product pages quietly record their handle to localStorage; a section placed elsewhere reads those handles back and re-fetches each one's live price/image from the Storefront API, so nothing shown is stale.
